# Optimization

Use this method when the objective is to move or preserve a measurable metric.
First establish the real measurement surface, baseline, variance, constraints,
and correctness invariants. Profile or otherwise gather evidence about the
dominant cost before committing to an intervention. Keep hypotheses
disposable, compare each candidate fairly, and stop when the evidence shows
the remaining ceiling is not worth the tradeoff.

The mission proposal owns all specialization. It defines metric assertions,
correctness assertions, coherent experiment tasks, and structured command
oracles drawn from the repository's reproducible measurement surfaces. Never
replace an independent measurement with a worker-authored claim, weaken the
workload, or optimize a proxy that does not decide the objective.

The stop bar is attested because improvement claims often combine measured
results with judgment about validity and tradeoffs. Use command proof wherever
an exit status decides the whole claim and independent judgment for comparison
fairness, generality, and quality. The gap review checks metric integrity,
regressions, and unexplained tradeoffs before finish.
