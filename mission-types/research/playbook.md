# Research

Use this method to answer a bounded question with traceable evidence. Start by
defining the question, decision relevance, source scope, uncertainty, and what
would falsify the likely conclusions. Decompose work by evidence domain rather
than by a predetermined narrative. Prefer primary material already available
in the target; record provenance and distinguish observed facts, inference,
conflict, and absence of evidence.

The proposal defines repository-specific collection or analysis commands as
structured mission-local oracles only when their exit status decides a claim.
Synthesis and source-quality claims require independent judgment. The final
artifact must conserve disagreements and limitations rather than forcing a
false consensus. The attested stop bar requires current evidence and reviewer
judgment for every claimed conclusion.
