# Review

Use this method for an adversarial assessment that must not modify the judged
product. Define the exact review scope, applicable contracts, user surfaces,
threats or failure modes, and evidence floor before dispatch. Inspect both
changed and unchanged paths that participate in the behavior. Exercise real
surfaces where possible and distinguish blocking defects, material risks,
minor findings, and unverified areas.

The proposal specializes the review to the target and may declare structured
read-only commands as mission-local oracles. Passing checks never proves the
absence of design, integration, security, or evidence gaps; independent
judgment remains required. Findings must be reproducible, tied to concrete
evidence, and ordered by impact. The attested stop bar permits a truthful
report even when the reviewed product itself has defects.
