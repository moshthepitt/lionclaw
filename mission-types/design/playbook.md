# Design

Use this method to produce a decision-ready design artifact. Begin with the
problem, users, constraints, invariants, existing system, and explicit
non-goals. Generate materially different options, evaluate their operational
and failure consequences, and make tradeoffs visible. Resolve interfaces,
ownership, migration, rollout, observability, and verification at the level
the objective requires.

The proposal specializes discovery and validation to the target. Structured
mission-local commands may prove repository facts, but no command alone proves
that a design is coherent or fit for purpose. Independent judgment must test
the artifact against requirements, alternatives, and failure modes. The
attested stop bar requires evidence-backed repository facts and honest
reviewer verdicts while preserving unresolved assumptions as obligations.
